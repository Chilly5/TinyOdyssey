About this map

A diorama of the Greek myths, set on the real Mediterranean. The coastlines, mountains and sea floor come from satellite elevation data. The whole scene is a 3D model made of small cubes, with every tree, town, road and temple in place, seen through one fixed camera. Each step of the zoom is its own picture of that model at a finer grain, so going closer does not enlarge anything: a house gains its door, then its shutters and the tiles of its roof. The sea, the ships, the monsters and the people going about their day are drawn live on top, and step behind the houses as they pass. Click any of them to learn who they are and what they are up to, and the map will follow them. A day lasts five minutes: at dusk the lamps come on and most people go indoors.

The picture comes in three styles: clean stacked terraces, pixel art, and an earlier version that an image model painted from the same scene. With the sound on, a song plays over the whole map with the wind. The wind drops as you come down, and only close to the ground do you hear the sea on the shore, the murmur of a town and the birds and insects of the country.

Where the ancient world argued about a location, which is most of the Odyssey, the map follows the tradition repeated most often and says so.

Terrain
SRTM, GMTED and ETOPO1 through Mapzen terrain tiles
Scale
1 pixel of the open country is about 40 m; heights are stretched roughly seven times; towns and landmarks are toy-sized on purpose
Painted style
Redrawn by FLUX.2 klein, an open image model, so treat its details as illustration.
People, ships and monsters
About 45 small voxel models and the landmarks' own moving parts, built and rendered in Blender
Sound
A song over everything. Under it a recording of a desert wind, two of market crowds for the towns, and sea, birds and insects made in the browser from noise. It is on from your first click or key press, which is as soon as a browser allows; the button or M turns it off, and that is remembered.
Keys
Arrows or WASD move, + and − zoom, V changes the style, N the time of day, M the sound. Click anyone on the map to follow them and hear who they are
Inspired by
hexagonal.earth and the isometric city maps of New York, London, Singapore and San Francisco

Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom, a long way. Click a name, or anyone you meet.
